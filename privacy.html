Privacy Policy

Atlas is a single-user personal application. It accesses the developer's own Google account data (calendar events and email metadata) solely to display that data back to the developer.

Data is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. Data is not used for advertising. Google user data is stored only on the developer's own device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled or access is revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
